4. Retention and deletion

Account information is retained to provide your account until you request deletion or the account is otherwise lawfully removed. There is currently no automatic inactivity deletion. Games and notes remain until deleted. You can delete individual reports in “My games”. To request deletion of your whole account and its games, email the controller above.

Deleted games are no longer included in the public projection or statistics calculated on the next request. We cannot recall public results that other people have already copied or downloaded.

Correspondence is retained while needed to handle the request, follow-up questions or the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims. Statutory retention duties remain applicable. Providers' security and operational records follow their product-specific retention periods and legal duties. Brevo is configured to retain transactional email logs for one month and not to store previews of new transactional emails. We also review associated email records when handling a deletion request.

Deleted information may remain in restricted backups until the relevant backup cycle expires. It is not used for ongoing operations; deletions must be reapplied if a backup is restored.

5. Storage on your device

The online app keeps the current sign-in in memory rather than persisting it in Local Storage. Reloading requires a fresh sign-in. An unsent game draft may be saved in the tab's Session Storage and is removed after a successful report or sign-out. You can also clear browser data yourself.

When installed as a PWA, a service worker may cache static offline files. That service worker does not cache account data or game API responses. Strictly necessary storage or access for a function you explicitly request is based on section 25(2)(2) TDDDG. The public meta page does not manage an account session.

Fonts and icons load from the Sensou website you visit. We do not connect to Google Fonts.

Survey results and reviewed written answers

The Meta Survey page publishes numerical summaries of the main Bushido survey and separately reviewed written answers. Direct personal references and identifying local/event context are removed where found and marked in the text. Published texts have independent document IDs, without respondent IDs, numeric response profiles, main faction, experience or submission time attached. Answers from the same submission are not publicly linked. Writing style or context can nevertheless be recognisable; this is not a guarantee that every possible inference is excluded.

The public numeric files contain no individual response rows. Small rating groups and some small nomination counts are withheld. For the matchup matrix and two-sided matchup view, each faction group needs at least five respondents; individual cells within an eligible group may have fewer nominations. Public texts, files and CSV downloads may be copied by visitors and cannot be recalled from their copies. Original responses and the private review mapping are not published. Newly added or changed text is withheld until reviewed separately.

The page does not connect to Tally or load advertising, consent or analytics tags. Participation is available through an external link to Tally, opened only when followed; the survey has its own participant information. The published snapshot and text-review dates are displayed. A scheduled Supabase server function retrieves the main survey from the Tally API hourly, processes responses in memory and publishes grouped statistics. Cloudflare delivers these public statistics to the browser. The API key is stored as an encrypted server secret; the review ledger is kept in a private Supabase bucket. New or changed written answers require a separate privacy review. The local response cache and internal dashboard remain private.

Before advertising is activated later, this policy will be updated and the necessary consent controls will be provided. This policy does not constitute consent to future advertising.

7. Your rights

Subject to the GDPR's conditions, you have rights to access (Article 15), rectification (Article 16), erasure (Article 17), restriction (Article 18) and data portability (Article 20). Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it at any time for the future; this does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.

Objection: under Article 21, you may object to processing based on legitimate interests on grounds relating to your particular situation. Contact the_ZJ@gmx.de. We may need proportionate identity checks to prevent unauthorised disclosure or deletion. Do not send passwords or confirmation/reset links.

You can complain to a data protection supervisory authority (Article 77), particularly where you usually live, work or believe an infringement took place. The authority responsible for the operator is the Saxon Data Protection and Transparency Commissioner.
